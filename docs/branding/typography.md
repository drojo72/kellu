# KELLU — Typography and wordmark

## 1. Source typeface selection

### Fraunces 72 pt Soft — selected base

- **Credits recorded during design:** undercase type, including Julián Rol, Sofía Mohr and collaborators.
- **Licence recorded during design:** SIL Open Font License (OFL).
- **Reason for selection:** soft organic terminals, visual warmth, and structural robustness for interface use.

Verify the exact release, source-file metadata, and accompanying OFL text before publishing.

### Other typefaces considered

| Typeface | Credits recorded during design | Reason not selected |
|---|---|---|
| Instrument Serif | Instrument and Rodrigo Fuenzalida | More formal/high-contrast appearance; reduced robustness at small sizes was a concern |
| Bitter | Sol Matas / Huerta Tipográfica | Robust slab-serif structure, but less fluid than the intended identity |

![Compared pre-selected fonts](/images/base-font-comparison.png)

These notes preserve the design rationale. Verify credits and version details against original typeface sources before publication.

## 2. Wordmark modifications

![Original versus modified wordmark](/images/base-font-modified-font-comparison.png)

KELLU is a modified design based on Fraunces 72 pt Soft, not simply unmodified text set in the source font.

Modifications recorded during design:
- Extended the vertical stems of `k` and both `l` letters to align with the isotype's upper boundary.
- Reduced the thickness of those extended stems to preserve refinement.
- Refined and slightly thinned the curves of `e` and `u`.
- Tightened the `k–e` spacing to remove excessive initial air without harming legibility.
- Adjusted the outer width of `u` and shifted its closing vertical left to improve its counter and balance the spacing against `ll`.
- Cleaned letter structure and overlaps and refined metrics/kerning.

Some edits were recorded as clicks in the font editor. Record final numeric glyph coordinates and metrics if exact reproducibility is required.
